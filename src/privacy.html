WhereSub

Privacy

WhereSub is a calculator and a memory, not a scanner. It only stores what you type, import, or export yourself.

What this extension does not do

  • It does not read your email, browsing history, cookies, or bank pages.
  • It does not send subscription data to a server.
  • It does not require an account.
  • It does not include analytics, ads, or remote fonts.

What it stores

Names, amounts, billing cycles, renewal dates, notes, and settings live in chrome.storage.local on this browser profile. You can export, import, or delete that data from the ledger.
